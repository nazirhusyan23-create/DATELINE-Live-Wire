About DATELINE

DATELINE started as a simple question: why does checking the news online usually mean wading through auto-playing videos, pop-up newsletters, and pages that take ten seconds to load just to show a headline? This site is the opposite — a fast, quiet, single-page reader built for scanning what's happening right now and jumping straight to the source when a story is worth reading in full.

Everything here is live. There's no editorial team picking stories, no algorithm trying to keep you scrolling — just a clean read on nine categories of public news, plus the local weather, updated in real time. Think of it as a wire desk: headlines in, links out, no noise in between.

DATELINE pulls live headlines from Google News' public RSS feeds across nine categories, plus live local weather. It's meant as a fast, distraction-free way to scan what's happening before clicking through to read the full story on the original publisher's site.
