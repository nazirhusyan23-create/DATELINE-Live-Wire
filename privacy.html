Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

DATELINE ("we", "this site") is a news reader that displays public RSS headlines from Google News. This page explains what data is collected when you use the site.

Information we collect

DATELINE itself has no backend database, no accounts, and no login. We do not collect names, emails, or any personal data directly.

Location & weather

If you allow it, your browser's geolocation (or, as a fallback, an approximate location from your IP address) is used only to show local weather. This is not stored — it's used for a single request to the Open-Meteo weather API each time you load the page.

Third-party services

This site loads content from and sends requests to: Google News (headlines), Open-Meteo (weather), and, if you have not granted location permission, an IP-geolocation lookup. Each of these providers may log standard request data (like your IP address) under their own privacy policies.

Advertising & cookies

This site works with Google AdSense, which may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Our approved ad-sellers are listed publicly in ads.txt. You can view or opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com, and see how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data retention

Because DATELINE has no accounts, database, or server-side logging of individual visits, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on request — we simply don't store it. Any "Saved" articles or notification preferences you set are kept only in your own browser's local storage and never leave your device.

Children's privacy

DATELINE is a general-audience news reader and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service embedded here, please contact us and we'll address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision — please check back periodically.
